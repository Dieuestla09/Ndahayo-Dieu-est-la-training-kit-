Why we exist

NDAHAYO DIEU EST LA started on the sideline of a five-a-side league, where half the squad played in mismatched shirts because proper kits were slow, overpriced, or built for people who don't actually play. We set out to fix that: real match-weight fabric, true team colourways, and sizing that fits players, not mannequins.

Today we outfit football, basketball, volleyball and athletics squads, plus everyday training kits for anyone who shows up to move. No filler collections — every product on this site is something we'd wear ourselves.

How we work
